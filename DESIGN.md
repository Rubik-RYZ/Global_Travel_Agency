## Brand & Style

TERRANOVA evokes the grandeur of 18th-century naturalist expeditions, botanical field journals, and bespoke luxury travel. The aesthetic marries historical scientific curiosity with sophisticated editorial refinement. It treats travel not as mass leisure, but as an aesthetic pilgrimage, slow exploration, and living cartography.

The design movement combines **Editorial Luxury** and **Tactile Parchment Materiality**:
- **Naturalist Ephemera:** Warm tinted paper textures, radial parchment stippling, botanical branch silhouettes, and postal/cartographic stamp motifs.
- **Editorial Typography:** Dramatic contrast between grand, high-contrast serif display titles (`Playfair Display`), functional clean modernist sans-serif metadata (`Work Sans`), and spontaneous expedition cursive (`Caveat`).
- **Organic Curvilinear Masks:** Custom botanical silhouette clip paths framing landscape imagery, softening harsh photographic grids into hand-cut specimen frames.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep evergreen foliage tones transitioning into sun-bleached parchment sheets.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural field elements: terracotta clay, deep forest canopy, aged cartographic parchment, and oxidized antique gold.

- **Primary (`#792609` / `#983d1f` / `#b85434`):** Terracotta and sealing wax red. Utilized for call-to-action buttons, chapter highlights, drop caps, and key interactive accents.
- **Secondary (`#141d16` / `#1b261d`):** Deep botanical forest green (Forest Night & Canopy). Used as the grounding canvas for the navigation bar, hero backdrop, inquiry banner, and search CTA button.
- **Tertiary (`#583e00` / `#c6a15b`):** Antique botanical gold. Applied to badges, compass elements, coordinates, and subtle navigational hover states.
- **Neutral (`#231a13` / `#3d322a` / `#1c1815`):** Warm iron-gall ink. Replaces harsh true blacks for body typography and metadata lines to sustain a printed book feel.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#faf5eb` / `#fff8f5`):** Warm expedition vellum and parchment, accented with `#ded2c1` hairline divider rules. Pure digital white (`#ffffff`) is strictly isolated to search input containers and badge backgrounds.

## Typography

The type hierarchy balances dramatic high-contrast serifs with restrained, functional sans-serif details and cursive field annotations:

- **Playfair Display:** Drives grand romantic display headlines, destination chapter titles, and editorial quotations. Italic styling is used selectively to emphasize key lyrical concepts (`HISTORIAS`, `ALMA NATURALISTA`).
- **Work Sans:** Serves all operational, transactional, and UI labeling tasks. Used with wide letter-spacing (`0.2em` to `0.3em`) in uppercase format for badges, coordinates, dates, and category tags.
- **Caveat (`cursive`):** Renders candid expedition field notes, directional handwritten callouts (*«Montañas que inspiran →»*, *«La dolce vita ↓»*), and margin annotations. Requires web font load: `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Caveat:wght@400..700&display=swap');`
- **Accents:** Drop caps styled at `3.4rem` initiate manifesto paragraphs, while subtle script gestures are paired with scientific specimen stamps and curved ink arrows.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured **fixed max-width container** model (`max-w-7xl` or `1280px`), inset with generous page padding (`1.5rem` on mobile, scaling to `3rem` on desktop).

- **Vertical Overlaps:** Sections do not sit on flat edge-to-edge divisions. Major panels bridge transitions with negative margins (such as the main parchment sheet overlapping the hero foliage by `-4rem` to `-6rem`), unified by large curvilinear border radiuses (`rounded-t-[2.5rem]` to `[3.5rem]`).
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** 3-column card layouts employ staggered vertical offsets (`md:mt-8` on card 2, `md:mt-2` on card 3) to mimic manually mounted cards in an expedition scrapbook rather than a rigid digital matrix.
- **Micro Rhythm:** Badges and metadata containers use tight 4px and 8px interior gaps, preserving high information density within expansive outer section whitespace (`6rem` to `7rem` between narrative chapters).

## Elevation & Depth

The design avoids modern synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on physical, naturalist-inspired depth:

- **Tonal Parchment Surfaces:** Depth is achieved by shifting background tone from deepest canopy (`#141d16` and `#1b261d`) to warm paper (`#faf5eb`), supplemented by subtle radial stippling that evokes tactile cotton rag paper.
- **Atmospheric Gradient Vignettes:** Multi-layered directional overlays (`to-r` and `to-t`) blend photography seamlessly into dark foliage frames.
- **Tactile Paper Floating:** Search widgets, floating mega-menus, and testimonial plaques utilize deep warm-tinted diffused shadows (`rgba(36, 44, 38, 0.14)` to `rgba(15, 22, 17, 0.35)`), mimicking heavy pressed stationery raised above an explorer's desk.
- **Glass Accents:** Translucent frosted overlays (`bg-white/5` with `backdrop-blur-sm` and faint `border-white/20`) highlight navigational chips and play controls without breaking the vintage palette.

## Shapes

The interface embraces pill-shaped geometric containers contrasted with hand-hewn organic contours:

- **Pill Geometry (`roundedness: 3`):** Buttons, badge pills, search capsules, and metadata pills feature full rounded profiles (`rounded-full` or `rounded-3xl` for cards).
- **Curvilinear Botanical Masks:** Specimen photography is bounded by organic polygon clip paths with varied radii (`28px` to `32px`), giving every destination card a distinct silhouette that mimics pressed flora or hand-torn botanical paper.
- **Curved Transitions:** Structural transitions between major atmospheric sheets employ generous top radii (`rounded-t-[2.5rem]` to `[3.5rem]`).

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), terracotta base (`#b85434`), uppercase text in Work Sans (`text-xs`), tracked wide (`tracking-wider`), white label accompanied by a directional arrow (`→`). Slight hover lift (`-translate-y-0.5`) with warm drop shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Full pill with delicate border (`border-white/40` or `border-amber-200/50`), translucent backdrop blur (`bg-white/5`), ivory typography.
- **Card Action Link:** Minimal inline link anchored by a 24px terracotta line dash (`w-6 h-[1.5px] bg-[#983d1f]`), transitioning to primary red on hover.

### Badges & Postal Stamps
- **Specimen Pill:** Small pill container (`px-3.5 py-1.5`) with fine hairline border, housing a miniature leaf or coordinate icon and tracking-expanded micro-label.
- **Cartographic Postal Stamp:** Circular or octagonal line art watermark stamped in muted sepia (`#7a6452`), displaying location names and expedition years with dashed borders.

### Field Notes & Handwritten Callouts
- Freehand annotations styled with `font-family: 'Caveat', cursive` tilted slightly (`-rotate-2` to `rotate-3`), accented by hand-drawn vector curved arrows indicating destinations and imagery.

### Search Capsule
- Unified floating bar composed of destination, date range, and traveler metadata fields divided by hairline parchment separators (`#ebdccb`).
- Concludes with a contrasting deep forest pill button (`#1b261d`).

### Specimen Destination Cards
- Framed in a light clay wrapper (`#d9cbba`), containing an organic silhouette masked image container that expands on hover (`scale-105` over 700ms).
- Card footer displays chapter numbering (`Capítulo I`), serif title, duration monospace tag, and a structured metadata panel (`#f2e7d7`) outlining season, lodging, and signature experience.

### Drop Caps & Editorial Quotes
- Testimonial and manifesto paragraphs begin with an oversized Playfair Display drop cap (`3.4rem`, `#983d1f`) floating left.
- Quotation cards feature large decorative quotes and warm paper card styling flanked by handwritten ink sign-offs.
